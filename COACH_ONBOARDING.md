# Welcome to Duelr — Coach Onboarding

Thanks for being verified and joining the Duelr coaching roster. This doc walks you through everything you need to do before your profile goes live and students can start booking you.

---

## What is Duelr?

Duelr is a matchmaking and coaching platform for League of Legends players. Students browse verified Masters+ coaches, book a session (30, 60, or 90 minutes), and pay through the platform. Once payment is confirmed, they receive your Discord handle to reach out and schedule.

Reply with the following so we can set up your listing:

## How Bookings Work

1. A student finds your profile and picks a session length (30 / 60 / 90 min)
2. They pay upfront through Stripe Checkout
3. You receive an email notification with the student's Riot ID, session length, and your net payout
4. The student receives your Discord handle — they'll message you to schedule

**You set the pace.** There's no calendar integration — you and the student agree on a time over Discord.

---

## Payout Structure

| | |
|---|---|
| Your rate | Set by you (e.g. $40/hr) |
| Platform fee | 20% |
| **Your payout** | **80% of the session fee** |

**Example:** A 60-minute session at $40/hr → student pays $40 → you receive $32.

Payouts are sent automatically by Stripe after each confirmed booking clears the holding period.

## What Students See

- Your verified rank badge (Master / Grandmaster / Challenger)
- Your champion pool and roles
- Your hourly rate and available session lengths
- Your bio

**They do not see** your Discord handle or contact email until after payment is confirmed.
